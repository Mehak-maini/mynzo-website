## Three-page batch and enquiry path

| Draft URL | Reader need | Content boundary | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/platform/biodiversity-monitoring` | Can this help monitor my forest or restoration project? | Habitat observations, field evidence, suitability and limitations; no automatic species inventory or complete ecological assessment promise | Discuss biodiversity monitoring |
| `/blog/biodiversity-metrics-for-restoration-projects` | Which measurements answer my restoration question? | Definitions, a measurement-selection framework and source-supported limits; distinct from a product walkthrough | Read the monitoring page or discuss a project |
| `/solutions/project-developers` | What should I commission, prepare and hand over? | Stage, boundaries, baseline, evidence gaps and scoping; no promise to finance, develop, certify or sell credits | Share a forest-monitoring project brief |

Use shared English pages with relevant Indian and international examples. Do not create near-duplicate country pages merely to repeat keywords. Crosslink this batch, the existing forest-monitoring/digital-MRV pages and relevant articles. Match visible answers with structured data and keep source links beside technical claims. Clear answers, crawlable text and accurate markup support discovery; they do not guarantee rankings, rich results or AI citations. [Google's AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)

The draft enquiry work carries an allowlisted `interest` and content `source` from page CTAs to `/get-started`. Visitors can choose an optional project focus. The form should include that focus and source in the accepted enquiry and the existing non-PII `generate_lead` event. Keep names, email addresses, company details and free-text messages out of analytics. Ask for geography, site extent, existing evidence and reporting purpose in the project brief; do not promise a free assessment or a turnaround time that has not been agreed.

`enquiry_source` identifies the content CTA used. It is not a substitute for GA4's session landing page or acquisition source. A successful API response, backend receipt, email delivery and GA4 receipt are separate checks. This document does not claim they have all passed.

## Practical measurement

1. **Establish actual data access.** Use the relevant Mynzo GSC property and GA4 property when available. Record the property, date range, filters and publication dates with every report. No property connection or account setting was changed in this research task.
2. **Measure discovery in GSC.** Group the three URLs and adjacent pages; report impressions, clicks, CTR and position by page/query. Separate branded queries from biodiversity, restoration, habitat and developer queries. Report India and the rest of the world separately, with US/GB breakouts when samples allow. Use the [GSC Performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553) and inspect indexability independently.
3. **Use complete comparisons.** After enough time has passed, compare a complete 28-day post-publication window with the preceding 28 days, excluding incomplete recent data. New URLs have no pre-release performance history; compare the topic group and show new-page totals instead of an invented percentage uplift. Record seasonal changes and other releases. Small counts require plain counts, not confident trend claims.
4. **Verify accepted enquiries.** Check the [GA4 `generate_lead` event](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/ga4/reference/events#generate_lead) once per accepted form response, with `project_focus` and `enquiry_source` limited to approved values. Confirm any needed event-scoped reporting dimensions in GA4. Do not count opens, clicks, failed requests or preview QA as received leads.
5. **Connect visits to qualified enquiries.** Report organic landing-page sessions and accepted enquiries together, then add the team's qualification outcome. A relevant lead has a real site/project, a monitoring need Mynzo can address, and enough context for a technical discussion. Keep this operational qualification outside public analytics and avoid putting personal details in reports.
6. **Keep AI visibility separate.** Log an observed engine, prompt, date and cited URL when an answer cites Mynzo. A selected prompt result is not a market-wide visibility score. Referrer traffic alone will miss some AI visits; it also does not prove an increase in citations.
