## Editorial decision

Target the informational query **agroforestry benefits**, with useful treatment of disadvantages, crop competition, income timing and measurement. Title: **Agroforestry Benefits, Tradeoffs and How to Measure Them**. The existing article is the appropriate place for this material; no competing benefits URL is added.

The previous text asserted 30 to 80 percent income increases without a source, called named species native without supporting sourcing, claimed individual-tree carbon tracking without field teams, and implied reliable credit income. The refresh removes those claims. It separates farm production, income, ecological outcomes, carbon stock and credit issuance.

This is an evidence-led farming guide with a contextual forest-monitoring enquiry. It is not a claim that Mynzo delivers agronomy, calculates every indicator in the table, issues credits, sources buyers or has completed the illustrated projects. India and global readers receive equal attention through local crop, water, market and tenure questions rather than a universal species or return recommendation. The biodiversity guide remains the detailed destination for habitat and species monitoring.
