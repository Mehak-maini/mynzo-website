## Primary sources verified this session

1. **GEO BON, Ecosystem Structure**: https://geobon.org/ebvs/working-groups/ecosystem-structure/ . Opened successfully. Supports treating ecosystem structure as one class of biodiversity variables and combining in situ and remotely sensed observations. It does not say canopy extent measures total biodiversity.
2. **ESA, RS4EBV project**: https://eo4society.esa.int/projects/due-innovator-iii-rs4ebv-remote-sensing-for-essential-biodiversity-variables/ . Opened successfully. Describes selected biophysical variables, functional-diversity proxies, local pilots and validation. Appropriate evidence for the need to define the measure and its validation context. Avoid presenting proxies as direct species counts.
3. **ESA, Four ways that space is changing ecosystem monitoring**, 4 June 2021: https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Preparing_for_the_Future/Space_for_Earth/Four_ways_that_space_is_changing_ecosystem_monitoring . Opened successfully. Documents habitat fragmentation, vegetation seasonality and canopy chlorophyll in pilot sites spanning different biomes. Examples include Germany, France, the Netherlands and the UK. Supports regional method examples, not Mynzo delivery claims.
4. **IIRS / ISRO, Geoweb Services, Biodiversity Information System**: https://www.iirs.gov.in/geoweb-services . Opened successfully. Page last updated 22 April 2022. Describes India's national landscape biodiversity work combining satellite vegetation information, modeled disturbance/fragmentation and georeferenced field plots. Cite as a documented Indian example, not a current live-data guarantee. BIS subportal functionality was not tested.
5. **NRSC Forestry & Ecology sensors**: https://www.nrsc.gov.in/nrscnew/Apps_Forestry_Ecology_sensors.php . Opened successfully. Forest type/density, temporal change, phenology, biomass/structure are listed applications. Does not establish which sensors Mynzo uses or promise species-level accuracy.
6. **NatureScot, Site condition monitoring**: https://www.nature.scot/professional-advice/protected-areas-and-species/protected-areas/site-condition-monitoring . Opened successfully. Describes feature-specific monitoring of habitats and species, management context, pressures and specialist field input. Useful for explaining that a site's ecological condition must be assessed against a defined feature and purpose. UK programme example, not a universal regulatory method.
7. **FAO, The road to restoration: A guide to identifying priorities and indicators for restoration monitoring**, 2019: https://www.fao.org/sustainable-forest-management/toolbox/tools/tool-detail/en/c/1255095/ . Opened successfully. Starts with goals, land use, constraints and priorities before selecting indicators. Combines local and remote sources according to need and resources. Good support for project scoping and monitoring design.
8. **FAO FERM**: https://ferm.fao.org/ . Opened successfully. FERM is an official UN Decade restoration monitoring framework and supports reporting areas under restoration. Its dashboard data are self-reported. It is not a Mynzo certification, site-level biodiversity certification or proof that area restored equals ecological recovery.
9. **USGS NDVI**: https://www.usgs.gov/landsat-missions/landsat-normalized-difference-vegetation-index and https://www.usgs.gov/special-topics/remote-sensing-phenology/science/ndvi-foundation-remote-sensing-phenology . Official search content retrieved in full for relevant definitions, but a direct page open returned 405 for the first. Search content says NDVI measures vegetation greenness; the phenology page discusses saturation and soil effects. Prefer the successfully opened GEO BON and ESA pages for the buyer page, and verify USGS through another read method if citing more detailed NDVI mechanics.
10. **SER Standards, third edition June 2026**: https://www.ser.org/page/SERStandards . Official search result retrieved the current page including new edition and DOI `10.1111/rec.70441`; repeated direct opens failed in the web tool. This is important freshness context: do not describe the 2019 edition as current. Root informed. Do not rely on uninspected detail from the full standard.

## Key language boundaries

- Biodiversity monitoring can include habitats, species and other ecological dimensions. Define what the proposed project will actually measure.
- Forest cover, habitat extent and habitat condition are related but different. A larger or greener canopy alone is insufficient to establish native species recovery.
- Modelled habitat suitability does not establish presence; one non-detection does not establish absence.
- Use repeated methods, comparable seasons, a documented boundary and dated observations. Record changes in survey effort and method.
- Any eDNA, acoustic, camera-trap or specialist species survey may be an ecological method example, but must not be described as an available Mynzo service or integration.
- India and global examples are planning scenarios or external documented practice, not Mynzo case studies.
- Reporting data does not automatically prove nature-positive outcomes, biodiversity net gain, certification or credit eligibility.
