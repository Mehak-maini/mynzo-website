RESTORATION MONITORING PLAN: VISIT AND ACTION CHECKLIST
Mynzo Carbon | 23 September 2026

Use this editable record with the team responsible for the site's ecology and
management. It is a planning aid, not a survey protocol, certification or
regulatory assessment. Select measurements and targets for the actual project.

BEFORE A VISIT
[ ] Confirm the ecological question and decision the visit should support.
[ ] Identify the site, treatment area and boundary version.
[ ] Make previous intervention and visit records available to the field team.
[ ] Confirm the method, sampling design, seasonal window and access arrangements.
[ ] Check who will review the evidence and who can authorise a management action.
[ ] Identify sensitive data and agree appropriate access and sharing.

BEFORE REPORTING
[ ] Distinguish work completed, establishment and ecological recovery.
[ ] Show observation dates, coverage and missing checks.
[ ] Keep original cohorts, replacements and recruits distinguishable.
[ ] Distinguish observations from interpretations and modelled estimates.
[ ] Check that units, boundaries and methods support the comparison made.
[ ] Record the evidence behind any management change.
[ ] Carry unresolved findings into the next team's handover.
