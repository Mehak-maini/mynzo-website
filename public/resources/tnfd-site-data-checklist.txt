TNFD SITE DATA CHECKLIST
Mynzo Carbon | 26 November 2026

Use this working checklist to prepare site records for your nature assessment team. Copy the site block per location and repeat the observation and question blocks as needed. Use the same site ID to link records. Keep source files in a controlled shared folder and review what may be shared externally.

HANDOVER CHECK
[ ] Trace one finding back to the original record, site and measurement dates.
[ ] Show sites without evidence and keep the coverage denominator visible.
[ ] Record boundary, method or unit changes before comparing periods.
[ ] Document where a location or result is approximate.
[ ] Verify the owners and due dates for follow-up checks.
[ ] Agree who can access confidential data and sensitive location records.
