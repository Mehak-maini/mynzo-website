4. COMPARABILITY AND REVIEW
Are boundaries, definitions and units consistent between periods?
Is survey effort comparable, and are seasonal differences recorded?
Are missing records separate from measured zero values?
Are non-detections labelled without assuming proven absence?
Are model estimates separate from direct observations?
Are native species and invasive species distinguishable in the results?
Are habitat extent and ecological condition reported separately?
Could weather, disturbance or method changes explain the result?
Who will review the ecological interpretation?
